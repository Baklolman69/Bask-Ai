# Best Use of AI for Feelio (Bask AI)

1. **Empathetic Reflection & Smart Feature Routing**: Instantly processes daily thoughts and mood logs using Groq AI (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile`) to deliver warm, non-judgmental validation and auto-route users to CBT, meditation, or journaling tools.
2. **Biometric & Health Intelligence**: Correlates real-time Wear OS smartwatch telemetry (BPM, SpO2, sleep hours) with emotional state to generate actionable lifestyle and stress-recovery insights.
3. **Cognitive Distortion & CBT Reframing**: Detects negative thought patterns (e.g., catastrophizing, overthinking) to guide users through structured, evidence-based cognitive reframing exercises.
4. **Adaptive Contextual Micro-Nudges**: Powers Android `WorkManager` background workers to deliver personalized hydration prompts and proactive stress interventions tuned to user habits.
5. **Privacy-First On-Device AI**: Combines low-latency cloud inference with local on-device fallback models (Gemini Nano) to guarantee total offline privacy and zero data exposure.
